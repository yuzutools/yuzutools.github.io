Xero keyboard shortcuts: the honest guide

Updated June 2026 · by Yuzu Tools

If you work in Xero all day, every extra click costs you minutes — and minutes add up. This page covers the keyboard shortcuts Xero actually ships, where they stop working, and how to drive the rest of Xero from the keyboard.

What's built into Xero

Xero ships essentially one global shortcut — search:

ShortcutWhat it does
/Opens Xero's global search from most screens. Type to find contacts, invoices and transactions, or type a screen's shortcut letter (for example i for invoices) and hit Enter to jump there. An "Add new" option appears below the search bar for quick creation.

The catch, per Xero's own documentation: search shortcuts do not work on the Fixed Assets, Reports, Projects, Expenses and Payroll screens — and the reports area is exactly where accountants spend much of their day.

What's missing

There are no shortcuts for jumping straight to a specific report, no user-definable hotkeys for the screens you open fifty times a day, and no quick way to switch between organisations — the single most repeated action for bookkeepers who manage several clients. Users have been asking for more keyboard navigation on Xero's product ideas forum for years.
