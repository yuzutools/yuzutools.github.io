Yuzu Toolbar for Procore — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 13, 2026

The short version

We collect nothing. We transmit nothing. Everything happens in your browser.

What the extension does

Yuzu Toolbar for Procore adds a command palette, keyboard shortcuts and a link sidebar to the Procore web application. When you pick an item, the extension simply opens the corresponding Procore page — exactly as if you had clicked through Procore's own menus.

Data collection

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, project data, or browsing history. It makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no telemetry, no remote servers, no accounts. It never reads your RFIs, drawings, budgets, directory entries, or any other content of your Procore account.

Settings

Your preferences are saved using Chrome's built-in storage.sync area: pinned palette items, whether the sidebar is open, and a small list of the projects you have opened (their numeric id and name, taken from the page title) so the palette can offer one-keystroke switching between them. This data stays inside your browser profile and, if you are signed into Chrome, is synced between your devices by Google under Google's privacy policy. We have no access to it, and you can remove any learned project on the extension's settings page.

Permissions

The extension requests the minimum it needs: access to Procore's own domains (*.procore.com) so the palette and sidebar can work on Procore pages, and storage for the settings above. It does not run on any other website.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address and the extension's listing will be updated. A change will never include collecting your data.
